Important: “keep Psi0 at 10 steps” applies to the unchanged scalar full-flow path. Setting every PI-R2 fast cycle to ten would make a legacy 24-action /act perform 40 rolling evaluations and erase much of the reactive sampler’s latency advantage.

Change boundary

Unchanged, added, and intentionally deferred

Unchanged from Psi0

  • Qwen3-VL and ActionTransformer learned parameters
  • Strict checkpoint key and tensor shapes
  • Original ten-step scalar full-flow scheduler
  • SIMPLE transforms and /act response contract
  • Original src/psi implementation

Added in Psi-R2

  • Per-position τ→σ schedule conversion
  • Explicit observation/state time at σ=1
  • Stateful rolling action/time buffers
  • Cached slow VLM and fresh-state fast paths
  • /slow, /fast, and health endpoints

Deferred to finetuning

  • PI-R2 ramp-schedule training distribution
  • Learned image-delay embedding
  • Stale-vision robustness training
  • Dynamic d from measured control latency
  • Dedicated GPU stream/process performance tuning
